Investments

My Money separates Investment Cash from actual Stocks and Mutual Funds. Money transferred from Bank/Cash can sit in Investment Cash until you actually buy an investment.

Simple flow: Bank/Cash → Investment Cash → buy Stocks or Mutual Funds. Market-price changes affect investment value and net worth, not Bank/Cash or Investment Cash.
